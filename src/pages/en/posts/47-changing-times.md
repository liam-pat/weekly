**Codex**  
Subscribed to GPT Plus; I'd rate the Codex assistant a 6–7 out of 10.  
AI iterates insanely fast—Cursor pushes updates basically twice a week, and Codex TUI is no different.  
Usage quota is plenty for me; haven't hit rate limits yet.  

* In terms of efficiency, it's really solid~
* In terms of value for money, it's definitely pricey~

<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2025/12/47lifegptplus.jpg" width="800" />

**Vibrant Greater Bay Area, Charming New Guangzhou**

Guangzhou hosted a ton of events in November and December: National Games, Garden Expo, Light Festival, National Para Games...  
Guangzhou still feels beautiful and pleasant to live in; I've loved this city since I was a kid.  
It feels like if someone isn't from Guangdong, it's hard to manage Guangdong well—yet neighboring Shenzhen does great...  
In terms of economy and infrastructure development, compared to when I was in middle or high school, it's really declined quite a bit~~
