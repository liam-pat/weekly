## Learning

**Jyutping (Cantonese Romanization)**  
<https://jyutping.org/>  
<https://corpus.eduhk.hk/cantonese/>  
iOS now supports LSHK Cantonese Jyutping input. To type Cantonese characters accurately, it's really worth learning—the first site summarizes everything wonderfully. The second link offers a free Cantonese learning course from The Education University of Hong Kong.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/09/13learningcantonesepinyin.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**Shuangpin (Double Pinyin)**  
<https://api.ihint.me/shuang/>  
iOS supports Shuangpin as well, which is slightly faster than standard full Pinyin. Wanted to learn it 2 years ago, but kept getting discouraged by comments saying "it's pointless and unnecessary." Whatever, I'll just learn it first and see!! I chose the Xiaohe layout.

## Reading

**How to Write Good Articles**  
Started reading this book—mentioned it previously and finally bought a copy to read. Very light and breezy, teaching you how to gather and accumulate writing material. It's so thin that you can finish it in a few days, and bookstores were completely sold out. Guess slim and concise books are the trend now; even at just a few dozen pages, it still cost 20 bucks.
