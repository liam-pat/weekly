**Monotony of Daily Life**  
Wondering if everyone else feels that days fly by so fast. The past few years have blurred past—10 years gone in the blink of an eye.  
The few moments that leave a memory each day now:  

* Morning commute (seeing different people, switching up routes)
* Lunchtime walk (routes aren't fixed either)
* Evening commute (seeing different faces, rotating between several routes)

I've talked about this before: if every single day is just repeating the same routine, what is the meaning of life? You might not even feel truly alive.  
The only thing I can do right now is try my best to find non-repetitive things to do within each day.
