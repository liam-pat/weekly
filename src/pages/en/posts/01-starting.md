## Interesting Tools

**AMD CPU TDP Page**  
<https://www.mydrivers.com/zhuanti/tianti/cpu/index_amd.html>  
A comprehensive list of AMD CPUs, making it super easy to compare TDP, core counts, and other specs.

**Validrive**  
<https://www.grc.com/validrive.htm>  
A tool that checks whether a USB drive has fake capacity. I was once duped by a fake TF card myself—scams like that really catch you off guard.
