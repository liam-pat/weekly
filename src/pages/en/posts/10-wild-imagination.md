**Chinese A-Shares**  
A-shares are in a league of their own, marching to their own beat. People's living standards have clearly improved, and the world says our nation is rising, yet the A-share market experience goes completely in reverse.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/08/10lifebiga.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**Garmin Watch**  
Picked up a Garmin watch ⌚️ this week to level up my running 🏃🏻‍♀️ and swimming 🏊🏻!! Previously logged workouts on iPhone, but the pain points were missing heart rate data, clumsy pauses, and annoying alert management.  
Took it for an easy 8 km test run that same evening—felt effortless! Kept my heart rate around 160 bpm, and being able to check real-time pace and heart rate to make quick adjustments helped me cruise at a 5'15" /km pace on a simple flat road.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/08/10lifegarmin.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**Greater Bay Area Art Center**
