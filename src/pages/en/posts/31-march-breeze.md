**Casual Musings**  
Haven't really been surfing the web (learning) much lately. Life has been tangled up in a pile of chores, feeling like I can see the end of the road from here—repeating the same routine day in and day out. Over the past two weeks I've thought a lot about trying to break through~  
Seems like a deadlock though. When time is gone, it's gone. You can only look forward, not back~  
Attended a middle school classmate's wedding this week and caught up with so many familiar faces~ Felt like traveling back to middle school and those carefree days. In the blink of an eye, everyone is having kids~~ People were discussing who just had twin girls, damn...  
Looking at classmates who had stellar grades back then, you'd think they'd be thriving the most, but reality isn't always like that. Instead, the ones who were most quiet and unassuming back then are now chatting up a storm with supreme confidence~~  
Always pondering the meaning of existence, frequently asking these philosophical questions~
