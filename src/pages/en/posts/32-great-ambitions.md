**China Mobile (CMCC) Broadband**  
Had a China Telecom broadband line (¥1680/100M/year) expire this month, so I switched to China Mobile 300M—only 1/3 the price. Telecom really makes users suffer with high prices. Haven't noticed any mobile network hiccups so far~  
After inquiring and comparing plans (applicable for 2025 Guangzhou Mobile), here's the breakdown:  

* Combined Bundle
    * 300M, minimum spend ¥58 (can combine up to 3 numbers to hit ¥28). Broadband ¥30/month.
    * 600M, minimum spend ¥98 (can combine up to 3 numbers to hit ¥68). Broadband ¥30/month.
    * 1000M, minimum spend ¥138 (can combine up to 3 numbers to hit ¥108). Broadband ¥30/month.

* Standalone Broadband
    * 1000M, ¥480/year; reverts to ¥120/month in the second year. You can re-apply for the annual plan before it expires, but seemed too troublesome for me so I passed.

* Installation Fees
    * ¥100 IPTV
    * ¥180 Wi-Fi Optical Modem + Router + Setup fee/year (need to contact customer support to cancel in year 2)
    * ¥130 Bill credit rebate

I went with the 300M plan:

1. Year 1: ¥150 installation + ¥360 broadband/year + ¥10 SIM fee/month (¥630/year total)
2. Year 2: ¥10 SIM fee/month + ¥30 broadband/month (¥480/year total)
