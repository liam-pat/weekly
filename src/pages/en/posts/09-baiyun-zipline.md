## Life

1. Make a habit of regularly meeting or catching up with someone more capable than yourself—it's the best way to keep your perspective sharp and ahead.
2. Read more books on psychology, philosophy, military strategy, and human nature to deeply understand fundamental laws and human behavior.
3. Practice public speaking and presentation skills—it's the fastest way to boost your presence and confidence.
4. Pick a new city or place to live for a while.
5. Communicate often with younger generations to maintain a youthful, curious mindset.
6. Maintain physical flexibility to stay youthful and vibrant.
