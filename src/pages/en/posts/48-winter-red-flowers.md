**Personal Reflections**

* Knowledge only turns into wisdom when it serves others and is put into practice!

When I was younger and hot-headed, I'd sometimes cut people off while they were speaking just to point out flaws in their points.  
Even if their proposal wasn't perfect, once you interrupt them, no matter how brilliant your own solution is, they'll just perceive you as showing off.  
As a result, great ideas were often ignored, and I'd end up needlessly rubbing people the wrong way.  
I was aware of this at the time, but always told myself, "We probably won't cross paths again anyway, so who cares if they take my advice?"  
Looking back now though: if you carry on like that, what if a key mentor or benefactor was in the room?  
Could you end up missing out on valuable opportunities simply because of your own tactlessness?  

Before speaking up, step into the other person's shoes to see things objectively—framing your thoughts differently might just make all the difference in getting them accepted~
