## Life

**How to Remove the Default ABC Input Method on macOS**  

* Install *PlistEdit Pro*
* `sudo open ~/Library/Preferences/com.apple.HIToolbox.plist`
* Remove the row with `value = ABC`

Now I only keep Sogou Input, and hitting Shift toggles between Chinese and English—much more convenient~
