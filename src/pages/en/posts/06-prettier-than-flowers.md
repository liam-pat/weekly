**Fruit Hunter**  

* Lychees: Coming to the very end of the season. Only *Guiwei* and *Xianjinfeng* varieties left, but prices are way too steep—can't afford them.
* Yingzui Peaches (Eagle Beak Peaches) 🍑: Bought about 5 jin (~2.5 kg) from Lianping. So many peaches, couldn't finish them all, and only cost ¥35.
* Passion Fruit: You MUST get the Qinmi No. 9 variety. It's pricey, but ridiculously sweet and delicious.
