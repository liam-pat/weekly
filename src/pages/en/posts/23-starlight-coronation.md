## Life

**Boredom**  
Nothing particularly exciting went down this week. Life can't be thrilling every single day, after all ahhhhh~  

**Guangzhou Marathon**  
The Guangzhou Marathon is right around the corner. Back then, I used to jump at every chance to sign up, but now I'm pretty indifferent. The race route is literally the exact route I run on daily anyway; plus, you have to register, pay entry fees, and weave through huge crowds where pacing is completely out of your hands. Even if you finish at a 4:00/km pace, the overall experience doesn't beat a solitary run. Not quite sure why that is~
