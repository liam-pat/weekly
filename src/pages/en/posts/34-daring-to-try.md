## Life

**Replacing the Oral-B iO7 Battery**  
Recently my Oral-B iO7 electric toothbrush wouldn't hold a charge—probably a false 100% readout with dead cells. Bought a replacement battery on Taobao, and the process was shockingly easy. Back to full health!  
* Unscrew the base of the 🪥 using the charging stand
* Pop out the coil clip at the top of the 🪥
* Push downwards, done

At first I thought it would be super tricky and might ruin the waterproofing, BUT once taken apart, it was remarkably simple—infinitely easier than replacing an iPhone battery~

**New Desk Time!**  
Found my old desk purchase history: bought it casually in January 2021 for ¥200+. Used it for over 4 years. Even though only one drawer broke, it felt too cramped—gave myself an excuse to upgrade~  

Components~  
* Custom solid wood tabletop  
* Standing desk motorized frame  

Extremely time-consuming, especially the communication. Originally looked at Loctek all-in-one desks on JD, but they didn't specify wood types and only had one solid wood option. So I opted for a custom tabletop. Spent an entire evening researching lumber alone.  

Tabletop construction types~  
* Composite laminate / engineered board (popular in Scandinavian style, resistant to warping)  
* Edge-glued solid wood planks (what people commonly call multi-plank, customizable materials)  
* Single solid slab (too expensive, not practical for average folks)  
* Others  

Weighing options between these wood types—even insiders can get tricked since domestic wood is often passed off as imported:  
* Oak (Red Oak / White Oak) -> Great for southern climates, high hardness, mold-resistant  
* Ash -> Relatively affordable, easy to work with  
* Cherry -> Has mineral streaks, relatively soft, darkens with age  
* Black Walnut -> Dark, premium aesthetic  

I ended up choosing a 5-plank red oak finished with a black walnut stain. If you want to know more, just ask ChatGPT—I'm only a semi-expert myself~~
