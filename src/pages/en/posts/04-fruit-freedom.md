**ChatGPT for Mac**  
<https://openai.com/chatgpt/mac/>  
The official ChatGPT Mac app is finally here. My previous workaround was running the web app inside MenubarX, which was pretty unstable. The native desktop app is definitely more stable, though bringing it up requires an extra step, and it doesn't dismiss automatically when losing focus—you have to hit `Cmd` + `W` to close it. Overall: MenubarX wins on quick interaction, but the native app wins on stability.

**Ditching the Glasses**  
Stumbled across this short book somewhere and jotted down these notes in Apple Notes~~

Actions:

* Blinking: once every 4 seconds
* Eye drops: close eyes tightly for 5 seconds after applying
* Eye rolls: left-right, up-down, clockwise and counterclockwise (10 reps each)
* Eye massage: standard eye exercises
* Focus training: track a pen tip or thumb back and forth
* Depth focus: alternate between near and far objects along the same sightline
* Eye splashing with water (not recommended by doctors due to tap water risks)

Foods:

* Carrots 🥕 (Beta-carotene converts to Vitamin A)
* Green vegetables 🥦 (Spinach, lutein, broccoli, kale)
* Dark chocolate 🍫 (Flavonoids)
* Red wine 🍷 (Flavonoids)
* Avocado 🥑
* Eggs 🥚 (Darker yolks are generally richer in nutrients)
* Garlic 🧄 (Antioxidants, boosts immunity)
* Sweet potatoes 🍠 (Beta-carotene)
* Oranges 🍊 (Vitamin C, antioxidant properties)
