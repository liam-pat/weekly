## Life

**OnePlus 11 Screen Replacement**  
My mom lost her phone due to being absent-minded. We filed a police report and got it back within half an hour, but the screen was smashed. The police said a young person brought it in and it was already broken.  
* The screen was hit in three spots—top, middle, bottom—which damaged the inner OLED panel, making touch unresponsive.
* What's the psychology behind deliberately smashing it? Did they think if it was broken, we'd take it to a nearby repair shop so someone could profit? Still can't wrap my head around that~
* Inquired at several Taobao shops and all quoted ¥900+. Samsung curved screens only come in OEM genuine parts. Finally took it to Guangzhou BuyNow computer market and paid ¥850 for a genuine replacement screen without warranty. Consider it paying money to ward off bad luck~
