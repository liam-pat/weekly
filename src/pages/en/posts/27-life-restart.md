## Life

**The Cable Speed Drop Mystery**  
My Shanze Ethernet cable started suffering frequent speed drops this year—plunging from 2.5G down to 100M. I spent ages troubleshooting, initially thinking it was an Intel i226 NIC compatibility issue. Turned out the problem was the cable itself: either oxidized RJ45 pins or the wire gauge was too thin. Won't buy ultra-thin flat cables just for neatness anymore. Crimping my own Cat 6 cables completely solved the speed drops.
