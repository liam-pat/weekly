## Life

**Reflections on Awakening**  
Came across an interesting takeaway:  
Only 1% of people achieve "true clarity / awakening" before age 30;  
Only 3% achieve it around their 30s ("establishing oneself");  
Only 8% achieve it after 50 ("knowing one's fate");  
Roughly 80% of people still haven't figured out their relationship with society or the true meaning of existence even after 50. So many spend their entire lives busy without truly knowing what they're living for. And even after awakening, many still cannot change their circumstances—they can only accept reality.
