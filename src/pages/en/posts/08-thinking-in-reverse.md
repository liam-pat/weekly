## Life

**A Few Words on OCR**  
> When converting printed paper text into digital format to read anywhere or reformat and reprint, the OCR accuracy on Mac Bob, iOS, and Android was just too error-prone. In the end, Quark OCR proved unbeatable. Non-subscribers only get 3 uses per day, but there's no limit on photo count per batch, which is plenty for daily use. No wonder students swear by it.
