## Interesting Tools

**QuantumultX Rules**   
<https://github.com/limbopro/Profiles4limbo>   
The main rule set I use on my iPhone. Pretty solid, set to active by default when on 5G network.   
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2025/06/41interestingtoolsqx.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="400" />

**Proxy Rules**  
<https://github.com/blackmatrix7/ios_rule_script/tree/master/rule>  
Community-maintained proxy rules. No need to write rules manually from scratch anymore—just include them.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2025/06/41interestingtoolsproxyrules.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**ColorHunt**  
<https://colorhunt.co/>    
Good color palettes keep your eyes from getting tired~~    
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2025/06/41interestingtoolscolorhunt.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**ClashX Meta**  
<https://github.com/MetaCubeX/ClashX.Meta>  
Currently using TUN on Mac. Previously used xray (via brew install). Recently found this much easier to use—all routing rules and policies can be handled in the UI;  
The best part is sharing a unified Clash config setup~     
* Android -> Flash | ClashMeta for Android (personally found the UI quite clunky)
* iPhone -> I use Quantumult X; if using Clash core, Stash seems worth trying
* Mac -> ClashX Meta
* Router -> PassWall (originally wanted to use Clash core, but gave up because it was too clunky)

## Life

**Halfway Through 2025**  

Before you know it, 2025 is already half over. How is everyone progressing on the goals set for this year? Time for a mid-year review~~   
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2025/06/41lifehalf25.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="400" />

**"With Buddha in Heart, Fear No 67"**  

A banner at my high school alma mater—cracked me up completely (Cantonese pun on keeping cool and steady).  
With the Gaokao (college entrance exam) wrapped up this year, I felt a wave of reflections~  

This high school wasn't originally my first choice. Back then I was supposed to go to a better high school next door, but due to some circumstances, I gave up that spot~~  
Because of that decision, I became who I am today. If not, would it have been an entirely different life? Who knows la.   
Sometimes an unassuming decision turns out to be a pivotal crossroads in life~~  

I often wonder if I made the wrong choice back then, or if I was just too playful?  
Having thoughts like this proves that I've gained some self-awareness~  

Looking back at those naive childhood thoughts really makes me chuckle~   
Anyway, it's in the past. Everyone has regrets and imperfections—those memories are what make life unforgettable, and reminiscing about them from time to time is actually quite delightful~
