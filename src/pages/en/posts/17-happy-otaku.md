## Interesting Tools

**Bibata Cursor**  
<https://github.com/ful1e5/Bibata_Cursor>  
Super cute mouse cursor set—looks really slick with the highlight effect.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/10/17interestingtoolscursor.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**NextTrace**  
<https://github.com/nxtrace/NTrace-core>  
Visual route tracking tool—much nicer and easier to use than standard traceroute.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/10/17interestingtoolsnexttrace.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**ImHex**  
<https://imhex.werwolv.net>  
A hex analysis and modification editor that looks incredibly cool. I remember when I was a kid watching someone open a file packed with raw hex data, tinker with a bunch of bytes, and suddenly play media from it.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/10/17interestingtoolsimhex.jpg?imageMogr2/format/webp" width="800" />

**Remote Working**   
<https://freshremote.work/>  
<https://www.upwork.com/>  
Sharing two platforms for finding remote gigs. Definitely make sure to find reliable clients—nearly all the gigs I took on over the past few years either fell apart midway or stiffed me on the final payment. Talk about terrible luck.
