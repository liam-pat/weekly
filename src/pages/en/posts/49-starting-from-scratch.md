## Learning 

**Is it time to upgrade your proxy?**
<https://sing-box.sagernet.org>
<https://wiki.metacubex.one>
<https://github.com/daeuniverse/dae>

Before:
- router: passwall
- server: xray
- ios: shadowrocket
- mac: clashx.meta

After:
- router: dae
- server: singbox + mihomo 
- ios: quantumultX + shadowrocket + stash
- android: Flclash
- mac: clashx.meta 

Years without maintenance plus the AI buff means technology iterates at breakneck speed.
Took advantage of the upgrade process to understand the pros and cons of each tool—whatever fits your workflow best is what matters most.
Also investigated a bunch of related tech concepts: IPv6 SLAAC, DH3, QUIC, Reality, anyTLS.

1. Currently still sticking with Reality + DoH.
2. DH3 is blazingly fast, but UDP keeps getting throttled. It ran reliably for a few months, but in the past month DH3 traffic was throttled hard, so I reverted to DoH.
3. Many providers are testing anyTLS now, but current compatibility is fairly average.

Sharing some config setups:
- mihomo <https://github.com/HenryChiao/mihomo_yamls>
- singbox <https://github.com/chika0801/sing-box-examples>
