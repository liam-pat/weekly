**MCP In Docker**  
<https://miantiao.me/posts/guide-to-running-mcp-server-in-a-sandbox/>  
Why isn't anyone running MCP inside Docker? Almost everyone uses local xnpx or uvx. Personally, I hate installing all these runtime environments locally—whenever I see this kind of setup, I immediately back off, even if I really want to try that MCP server;  
To be honest, running xnpx locally is messy and downright [insecure](https://invariantlabs.ai/blog/mcp-security-notification-tool-poisoning-attacks).  
Just seeing it puts me off. Finally someone spoke up and questioned this setup~~~
