## Interesting Tools

**Notion Avatar**  
<https://notion-avatar.vercel.app/>  
A Notion-style avatar creator. Already using it—swapped my Google avatar to one made with this.

**Ugly Avatar**  
<https://txstc55.github.io/ugly-avatar/>  
V2EX-style goofy/ugly avatars.

**Hotkey cheatsheet**  
<https://hotkeycheatsheet.com>  
Super handy when switching from Mac to Windows. When I only remember Mac shortcuts and have no clue about Windows ones, I just look them up here.

**Calligrapher**  
<https://www.calligrapher.ai/>  
AI-generated handwritten signatures. Generates randomly—if you spot one you like, just grab a screenshot.

**AI Find Location By Image**  
<https://geospy.ai>  
Find locations based on an image. Tested it out and the match with the actual photo location was surprisingly accurate—even coordinates within China were spot on.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/07/toolimageclearer.webp" width="800" />

**AI Make Image Clearer**  
<https://bigjpg.com>  
Make old or low-res photos crisper and clearer.

**Website Color Palette Recommender**  
<https://www.happyhues.co>  
Curates color palette recommendations for building apps and websites. An awesome tool for developers.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/07/toolpickupcolors.webp" width="800" />

**Remove Useless OBJ From Image**  
<https://inpaintweb.lxfater.com>  
Removes unwanted objects from images. Found the results pretty mediocre, honestly.

**Text 2 Favicon**  
<https://favicon.io>  
A site for generating website favicons. The favicons for my websites were all made here.

**Screen Website**  
<https://screen.guru>  
Takes clean screenshots of websites. Works pretty well~  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/07/toolcapweb.webp" width="800" />

**Screen On**  
<https://www.keepscreenon.com>  
<https://keep-screen-on.com>  
Keeps your screen awake and prevents it from sleeping or turning off. Great little tool when you don't want your computer to sleep but also don't want to mess with system power settings.  
<img src="https://gz-blog-storage-1252787757.cos.ap-guangzhou.myqcloud.com/weekly/2024/07/toolscreenon.webp" width="800" />

**Figma**  
<https://www.figma.com/>  
Designing in Figma is quite nice, especially friendly for iOS UI design.

### Life

**Swimming**
