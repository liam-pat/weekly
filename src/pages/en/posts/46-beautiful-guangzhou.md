## Life

**iPhone Repair**  

In October on my way from Tianjin to Guangzhou, my trusty iPhone 13 Pro Max of several years suddenly suffered the "green screen of death" at the airport. Took it straight to the Apple Store Genius Bar:  

* Screen issue; just needs a replacement. Since it's out of warranty, replacing it costs about CNY 2,800 (had AppleCare+, but of course it broke right after expiration~). 
* Any guarantee it won't turn green again after replacement? Nope. They explained all iPhone OLED screens have this risk and wouldn't admit it's a known defect~ 
* Can I trade it in? Genius Bar said they don't accept devices with broken screens and told me to find third-party buyers outside.  
* In the end, a friend bought it off me for 2,100.  

Honestly, I kind of expected this outcome, just wanted to see if there was any glimmer of hope~    
This is a known issue for this model, not user damage. Overseas they often replace screens for free;   
In mainland China, no such luck. I shared my thoughts on Apple's service quality in the mainland years ago.  

**OnePlus 12 Repair**

A few months ago, I spotted two black dots on my OnePlus 12. Saw plenty of similar cases on Xiaohongshu—it was a panel defect (liquid leakage). So I went to OPPO after-sales service: 

* Online booking made easy with customer support helping schedule the appointment.  
* An hour beforehand, the tech called to confirm the phone model and issue, checking parts inventory in advance (to prevent a wasted trip).  
* Arrived at the store, showed them the phone directly, mentioned it's a backup phone with zero drops or water damage (tech immediately logged it for screen replacement). 
* Tech said it could be repaired out-of-warranty, but needed manager sign-off (standard service jargon, really). 
* Screen was swapped out over lunchtime, they applied a fresh screen protector for free, and gave a 6-month warranty on the screen. 

Their service and accountability were so much better than Apple's. 
Not sure if Apple got burned too much by Huaqiangbei scalpers/repair exploits, but for a tech giant to degrade the service experience for everyday consumers just to fend off arbitrage feels like a net loss~
