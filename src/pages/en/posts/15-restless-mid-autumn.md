**Shunde Food & Culture**  
Took advantage of the Mid-Autumn break to head over to Shunde for great food and to soak in traditional culture.  
* Shunde truly has an insane amount of delicacies! Pretty much any shop run by Cantonese locals serves delicious food, and the prices are super reasonable.
* Traditional cultural heritage is preserved remarkably well!!!!
