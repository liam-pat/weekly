**Assembling the Big Toy**  
My custom big toy arrived right at the end of May—just before Children's Day on June 1st. Without realizing it, Children's Day has become such a distant memory~~  

* Tabletop: 20kg
* Standing desk motor frame: 32kg

Overall quite satisfied. The only slight downside is when the motor goes all the way up, giving it a firm push side-to-side still has a bit of wobble;  
The seller included caster wheels, which I'm super happy with—otherwise handling this weight alone would really be quite a struggle

**Always Forgetting What I've Learned?**  
There are plenty of great tutorials & articles in my Chrome bookmarks. In fact, I've already studied and read many of them, but I always forget that I've already seen them;  

To fix this, after reading something, I delete it from bookmarks and create a new page in Notion to record that I studied it;  
That way, when revisiting later, finding that page tells me when I learned that knowledge. If forgotten, time to refresh la ~~
